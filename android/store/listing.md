## Full description (1936/4000)

```text
Drop a gem into the jar. When two of the same touch, they merge into a bigger one. Keep merging, from a little pebble all the way up to the Diamond, before the jar fills to the top.

Can you make the Diamond?

HOW YOU PLAY
• Tap where you want the gem to fall. That's all it takes: one finger, no timer.
• Merge two of a kind to make the next gem up. Chain merges into combos for big points.
• Plan ahead: you can see the next gem coming.
• Squishy pops, sparkles and a cheerful narrator for every big combo: SWEET! JUICY! INCREDIBLE!

11 GEM SETS TO COLLECT, 132 GEMS IN ALL
Rocks & Gems, Crystal Cave, Ocean Jewels, Royal Treasury, Cosmic Gems, Volcano Gems, Pixel Treasure, Odd Shapes, Cyber Circuit, Neon Nights and Gem Pets. Each set turns every gem in the jar into something new, from glowing neon shapes to cute pets made of crystal. Open a set by making gems, or by winning stars in the Gem Mine. Can you open them all?

THE GEM MINE
• 20 puzzle stages deep under the ground, with crates to break, shelves, pegs, slopes and funnels in the jar.
• A set number of drops in every stage: plan your moves and win up to 3 stars.
• Boss stages with extra rewards, and rare gem sets that open with the stars you find.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A daily challenge and new quests every few hours.
• Gem Rush: how high can you score in 90 seconds?
• 200 levels to climb, with rewards, titles and new jar colours along the way.
• Boosters: Zap a gem away, or use a Star to lift any gem one level up.

MADE TO RELAX
• Plays offline, with no account and no sign-in.
• Your game is saved on your phone, and you can pick up right where you left off.
• Calm music, gentle buzzes and sounds you can turn off any time.

Gem Jar shows ads. An optional one-time "No Ads" purchase switches off the banner and the ads between games.

Privacy policy: https://www.sinadehesh.com/gemjar/privacy/
Terms of Service: https://www.sinadehesh.com/gemjar/terms/
```
