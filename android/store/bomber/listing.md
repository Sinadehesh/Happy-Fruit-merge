# Zombie Bomber – Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**. Lengths are within Play's limits
(checked when this file was written). Words like "best", "#1" or "free" are left out of the name and short description,
and out of all the graphics: Play rejects them there. Every number below is what the game has.

## Full description (3180/4000)

```text
Zombies are marching on your base. Drop bombs into the bomb bay, merge two of a kind into a bigger one, then drag it out onto the road and watch the horde fly.

From a little Firecracker all the way up to the Mega Nuke and the Black Hole: how big can you go?

HOW YOU PLAY
• Tap the bomb bay to drop a bomb. Two of the same merge into a bigger one, and quick merges chain into combos.
• Drag any bomb up onto the battlefield and let go: it's lobbed right there. A ring shows the blast, and a counter shows how many zombies it will take out.
• Wait for the horde to bunch up, then blow up a whole pack at once: TRIPLE KILL, MONSTER KILL... ZOMBIEGEDDON!
• Your soldiers fire from the base too. Keep the horde away from the gate.

12 BOMBS TO MERGE
Firecracker, Cherry Bomb, Grenade, Dynamite, TNT Barrel, Cluster Bomb, Aerial Bomb, Napalm, Bunker Buster, Thermobaric, Mega Nuke and Black Hole. The big ones have tricks: the Cluster Bomb splits in five, Napalm leaves a wall of fire, the Bunker Buster digs in and erupts, the Thermobaric goes off twice, the Mega Nuke makes a mushroom cloud and the Black Hole pulls the horde together before it blows.

12 KINDS OF ZOMBIE, KINGS AND BIG BOSSES
Walkers, Runners and Brutes at first, then Troopers in helmets, Sprinters, Bloaters that burst, Screamers that speed the others up, Hulks, glowing Ghouls, armored Juggernauts and giant Titans. Every 5th level a Zombie King leads the final wave, and later levels bring more than one. Every 25th level a big boss leads it instead, each with a trick of its own: the Butcher charges with its cleaver, the Broodmother hatches zombies as she walks, the Iron King's armor has to be blasted off first, and the Abomination splits in two.

500 LEVELS IN 25 ZONES
• From The Outskirts to Zero Hour, each zone with its own sky, light and weather: rain and lightning, snow and blizzards, sandstorms, neon rain, falling ash, hellfire and the aurora.
• Special levels: Coin Rush, The Swarm, Blackout (the lights go out), Graveyard Shift (they climb out of the ground), Lightning Storm and the Armored Column.
• The game adapts to how you play, so it stays a challenge without walls.
• Boosters: call in an Air Strike to bomb the whole road, or Freeze the horde for 6 seconds.
• Supply crates come down by parachute: tap one for coins, a booster or gems.
• Win streaks pay extra coins.

UPGRADE YOUR DEFENSES
Spend your coins on more soldiers (with a new look at every level), stronger weapons, a tougher base and bigger bombs.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A daily reward that grows over 7 days.
• 3 daily missions, and a bonus chest for doing all three.
• A free spin of the Lucky Wheel every day.
• 13 achievements to earn, and coins your soldiers collect while you're away.

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Plays offline, with no account and no sign-in.
• Your game is saved on your phone.

Zombie Bomber shows ads. Some rewards can be doubled by watching an ad, always your choice. Optional purchases: "No Ads" (one time) switches off the banner and the ads between levels, and gems come in packs. Reminder notifications are optional.

Privacy policy: https://sinadehesh.github.io/Happy-Fruit-merge/bomber/privacy.html
```

- Privacy policy: https://sinadehesh.github.io/Happy-Fruit-merge/bomber/privacy.html
- Contains ads: yes. In-app purchases: yes (No Ads `remove_ads`, a starter pack and 4 gem packs: see the README).
- Content rating: cartoon violence (zombies blown up, no blood). Simulated gambling: no (the Lucky Wheel and

- Data safety: see the privacy policy. Google AdMob collects the device's advertising ID, rough location from
  the IP address, app interactions and diagnostics; purchases go through Google Play; the game itself collects nothing.
