Gem Jar privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Gem Jar has no accounts and no analytics, and your game is saved only on your phone; we never receive it. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them. The optional No Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

This policy covers Gem Jar, a gem-merging puzzle game, for Android (package com.sinadehesh.gemjar) and its web version. “We” means Gem Jar’s developer, the developer named on Gem Jar’s Google Play page. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

What the game accesses on your phone

Gem Jar has no sign-in, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. On Android it uses:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to reach Google Play for the No Ads purchase. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration: the small buzzes when gems drop and merge. You can switch it off in the game’s Settings.
  • The advertising ID and Android’s ad services: used by Google AdMob, as described under “Ads”.
  • Google Play billing: for the No Ads purchase.

Google’s ads library also adds permissions to finish short background tasks (keeping the phone awake for a moment); the game itself does not use them.

What is stored, where, and for how long

The game saves these in its private storage on your phone (in the web version, in your browser’s storage for the game’s site):

  • Your game: the game in progress, best scores, your level and points, the gems you have made and the gem sets you have opened, Gem Mine stages and stars, boosters, quests and daily rewards.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, voice and vibration.

We never receive any of it. It stays until you delete it: uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings (or clearing the site’s data in your browser) erases everything.

What is collected and shared

We, the developer, do not receive any data about you: the game sends neither your game nor anything about you to us, and we do not sell data. The game contains no analytics. The one third-party code that collects data is Google AdMob, described below.

The No Ads purchase

No Ads is an optional one-time purchase sold through Google Play. It switches off the banner and the full-screen ads; the ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available. Google Play processes the payment under Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); Gem Jar never sees your card or payment details. The game asks Google Play only whether you own No Ads, so it can switch the ads off, also on a new phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a banner under the game;
  • a full-screen ad between games, at most once a minute, never during a game or a Gem Mine stage, and none in the first three Gem Mine stages;
  • short videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as keeping a game going when the jar fills up, a booster, more drops in the Gem Mine, another spin of the Lucky Wheel, a doubled gift, or double points for ten minutes. These play only when you tap to watch one.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can give a rough idea of where you are (your region, not your exact location);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as your device model and system version, and how the app and ads perform.

On newer Android phones, ads may also use Android’s privacy-preserving ad features (topics and ad measurement), which you can turn off in your phone’s Settings (Security & privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads). Google sends this data encrypted and handles it under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Ads may be personalised. Where the law requires it (in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game asks for your choice first, and you can change it at any time in the game’s Settings (“Privacy”). Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings. The web version shows no real ads.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the game; see GitHub’s privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The game itself keeps your progress in your browser and sends nothing to us.

Security

Your game data never leaves your phone and is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read Gem Jar’s private storage. The ad data Google collects is sent encrypted.

Your choices

You can stop playing, skip any ad you are offered to watch, or uninstall the game at any time, and change your ad choices as described under “Ads”. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request; for data Google holds, use Google’s tools at https://myaccount.google.com. Your game is only on your phone, so if it is deleted we cannot bring it back.

Children

Gem Jar is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Questions about privacy: use the contact email on Gem Jar’s Google Play listing, or open an issue at https://github.com/Sinadehesh/Happy-Fruit-merge/issues.
