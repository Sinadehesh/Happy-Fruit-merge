Gem Jar: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the Gem Jar app for Android (package com.sinadehesh.gemjar), published on Google Play by the developer named on its Google Play listing ("we"). It explains what information the app and the services it uses collect, and why.

What the game itself keeps

Gem Jar has no accounts and asks for no personal details. Your best score, the game in progress and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner under the game, a full-screen ad between games, and, if you choose, a short ad you can watch to keep going when the jar fills up. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app (for example model, operating system, language, crashes and performance);
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps).

This information is handled by Google under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app asks for your choice about personalised ads before showing them. You can change that choice at any time in the game: Settings, then Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads on most phones).

The "No Ads" purchase

You can buy "No Ads" once, through Google Play, to switch off the banner and full-screen ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own "No Ads", so it can restore it on a new phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and talk to Google Play. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration: for the small buzzes when you drop and merge gems. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Google Play billing: for the "No Ads" purchase.

Children

Gem Jar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For any question about privacy in Gem Jar, contact us at the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
