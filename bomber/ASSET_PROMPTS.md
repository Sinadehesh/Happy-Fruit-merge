# Zombie Bomber – asset prompts

Generate each sheet separately and send it over; it gets cut and wired into the game.

## 1. Bombs (12 levels)

```
A sprite sheet of 12 cartoon bombs for a mobile merge game, in a neat grid of 3 rows and 4 columns on a plain pure white background. Each bomb is separate, centered in its own cell, evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping.

IMPORTANT: every bomb is ROUND (a ball shape that fills its cell), with a short fuse or cap on top. Every bomb has a different main color and a different decoration so no two look alike. No faces.

STYLE: ultra-saturated glossy candy style, chunky and fat, thick soft outlines in a darker shade of each bomb's color, big white shiny highlights, juicy hard-candy surfaces, clean premium mobile-game icon art.

ITEMS, from tiny to apocalyptic, each clearly more powerful than the one before:
1. Firecracker ball, hot pink with a yellow band, tiny sparkling fuse
2. Cherry bomb, deep cherry red with a little green leaf by the fuse
3. Grenade, round, army green with a grid pattern and a pull-ring pin
4. Dynamite ball, orange-red bundle wrapped with a black strap
5. TNT barrel bomb, round, red with a yellow "TNT" band
6. Cluster bomb, electric blue with white dots of mini-bombs
7. Aerial bomb, steel grey with yellow stripes and rivets
8. Napalm bomb, bright orange with a black flame symbol
9. Bunker buster, black with orange hazard stripes
10. Thermobaric bomb, grape purple with a white skull symbol
11. Mega nuke, glowing yellow with a black radiation symbol
12. Black hole bomb, deep space purple-black with a swirling magenta vortex and a small soft glow (the final, ultimate bomb)

AVOID: text other than "TNT", numbers, logos, grid lines, shadows or glows on the background, realistic style, items touching, items cut off.
```

1. Walker: green skin, torn blue shirt, dark pants, average size
2. Runner: lime-yellow skin, torn red tank top, skinny and fast-looking, leaning forward
3. Brute: dark green, huge and muscular, torn purple shirt, slow and heavy
